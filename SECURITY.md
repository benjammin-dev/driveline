# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please **do not** open a public issue for security problems.

Report privately via GitHub's "Report a vulnerability" (Security → Advisories) on this repository, or by email to the address listed in the app's store listing once published.

Include: what you found, steps to reproduce, and the impact you believe it has. We aim to acknowledge reports within 3 business days and to share a fix timeline within 10 business days.

## Scope

The Driveline mobile apps and the Driveline AWS backend. Third-party services (Apple, Google, RevenueCat, Expo, AWS itself) should be reported to those vendors.

See [docs/04-security-privacy.md](docs/04-security-privacy.md) for how we protect user data.
